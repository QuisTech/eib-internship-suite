# EIB Stratoc Limited • SIWES & Internship Documentation Portal

A professional corporate documentation suite tailored to Nigerian tertiary institution standards (**ITF / SIWES Compliant**). Built for **EIB Stratoc Limited** (Member of EIB Group).

## Features
- **Official Letter of Completion & Clearance**: Formatted on company corporate letterhead with RC Number, corporate metadata, customizable addressee (SIWES Coordinator / HOD), and formal commendation.
- **Employer's Evaluation & Assessment Form (SIWES Form 8 Equivalent)**: 7-metric appraisal matrix, dynamic score calculation, distinction badges, and qualitative remarks.
- **Certificate of Industrial Training**: High-resolution ceremonial certificate with ornate borders, gold seal medallion, and official accreditation.
- **Authentic Stamp & Signature**: High-resolution digital recreation of the official **EIB GROUP ("ALWAYS ON TIME")** ink stamp and HR Marquis signature, plus photo stamp toggle.
- **Universal Direct Page Editing**: Click or double-click any field on the paper to edit directly with 2-way live synchronization.
- **1-Click Print / PDF**: Pixel-perfect A4 printing with dedicated `@media print` rules.
- **Word (.docx) Export**: Pre-generated Microsoft Word documents included.

## Technologies
- Pure Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
- Vector graphics, Canvas & high-definition asset pipeline
- Python `python-docx` for Word document generation

## Deployment
Deployed on Vercel Production.
